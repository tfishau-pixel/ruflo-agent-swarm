## Tools

### **SF Symbols**
- Library of 7,000+ symbols for Apple platforms
- Available: SF Symbols 8 beta, SF Symbols 7
- Requires macOS Sonoma or later

### **Icon Composer**
- Create layered icons with Liquid Glass
- Multi-layer icon format across appearance modes
- Requires macOS Sequoia or later

### **Parallax Image Tools**
- Parallax Previewer App (v2 for macOS 13.5+, v1 for macOS 10.10.5+)
- Parallax Exporter Plug-In for Adobe Photoshop (macOS & Windows)
- For tvOS and visionOS visual effects
