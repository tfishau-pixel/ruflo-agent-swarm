# Rules — vault/06 AI Workspace/ (trust boundary)

- All **unreviewed AI output** belongs here by default: research to
  `Research/`, drafts to `Drafts/`, triage to `AI Inbox/`, material ready for
  the owner's judgement to `Pending Review/`.
- Notes created here should state what they are: mark sourced facts,
  synthesis, and inference distinctly, and cite the notes/sources used.
  Add `status: pending-review` when the owner needs to look at it.
- AI may write directly to trusted folders (01–05) only when: formatting
  existing material, linking existing facts, updating explicitly authorised
  project state, or performing an explicitly requested edit. New conclusions
  never skip this workspace.
- Promotion out of this folder is the **owner's** call (or explicitly
  delegated). When promoting, merge into the correct source of truth, keep a
  provenance line ("AI synthesis from X, reviewed YYYY-MM-DD"), and remove
  the workspace copy only after the owner approves.
- Content here must never be treated as established fact when answering
  questions from the vault — cite it as "unreviewed AI material" if used.
