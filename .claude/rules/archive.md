# Rules — vault/99 Archive/

- The archive is **append-only in spirit**: material is moved here, not
  deleted, and is not edited except to fix broken links caused by the move.
- Archiving anything requires the `/archive-project` workflow or explicit
  instruction; before a project moves here, its final outcome, key decisions,
  and reusable knowledge must be preserved and discoverable (knowledge
  promoted to `03 Knowledge/`, decisions linked, remaining actions resolved
  or explicitly flagged).
- Keep archived projects intact as folders so history stays readable.
- Never "clean up" the archive by deleting old material; it is the vault's
  long-term memory.
- Restoring is always allowed: move the folder back to `01 Projects/` and
  update its README status.
