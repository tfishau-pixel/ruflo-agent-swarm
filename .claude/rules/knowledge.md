# Rules — vault/03 Knowledge/

- Notes here are **accepted, trusted knowledge**. New AI conclusions do not
  land here directly — they go to `vault/06 AI Workspace/` for review first.
- Before creating a note, search titles, aliases, and content; extend an
  existing note when the information belongs there. Fragmenting knowledge
  across near-duplicate notes is the main failure mode to avoid.
- Filenames are descriptive noun phrases a human would search for
  (e.g. `BMW E61 Cooling System.md`), not codes or dates.
- A note is as small as useful and as large as necessary; split only when it
  materially improves reuse, linking, comprehension, or retrieval.
- Record where knowledge came from (`source:` property or a Sources section)
  — future readers must be able to tell user experience from external
  sources from AI synthesis.
- Link to related projects, areas, and other knowledge when the relationship
  will matter later; never link merely because words co-occur.
