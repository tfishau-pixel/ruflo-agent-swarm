## Key numbers (for quick recall)

30 min hard straining without progress = ring the vet (10–15 min given ~1.5 h vet distance) · first suck ≤ 2 h · colostrum 50 ml/kg first feed, ~200 ml/kg per 24 h · lamb normal 39–40 °C, severe hypothermia < 37 °C, > 5 h old = glucose before warming · placenta normal ≤ 6 h, vet at 18 h.
