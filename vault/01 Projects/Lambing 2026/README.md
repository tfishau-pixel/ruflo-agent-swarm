# Lambing 2026

## Important Context
- **Distance rule:** normal advice is "ring the vet at 30 min hard straining without progress" — at ~1.5 h from help, ring at **10–15 min**. Early contact over DIY heroics, throughout.
- **Key numbers:** first suck ≤ 2 h · colostrum 50 ml/kg first feed, ~200 ml/kg per 24 h · lamb normal 39–40 °C, severe hypothermia < 37 °C, and > 5 h old = glucose **before** warming · placenta normal ≤ 6 h, vet at 18 h.
- Canonical guides live in the repo at `docs/lambing/` — the vault copies (once PR #6 merges) are for reading and offline access, not a competing source of truth.
